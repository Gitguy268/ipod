## [Unreleased]

### Changes since forking

This repository is a fork of [tvillarete/ipod-classic-js](https://github.com/tvillarete/ipod-classic-js) (MIT).

#### Added

- A music library read from a folder on your own computer, with tags and artwork kept in the browser.
- Album pages, search, playlists and genres.
- A Visualizer menu with two full-screen visualizers that follow the music: MilkDrop ([butterchurn](https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn); the center button changes the preset) and Bars (a spectrum after [Musializer](https://github.com/tsoding/musializer)). Once either has been opened, playback runs through Web Audio until the page is reloaded.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, `SECURITY.md`, issue and pull request templates, CI and a Dependabot configuration.

#### Removed

- The Apple Music and Spotify integrations.
